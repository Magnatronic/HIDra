========================================
HIDra v1.5 - Quick Reference Guide
========================================

WHAT IS THIS?
-------------
HIDra lets you control your computer with an Xbox controller.
Perfect for users who cannot use a mouse or keyboard.

GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Plug in your Xbox controller (USB or wireless adapter)
2. Double-click HIDra.UI.exe
3. The app connects automatically - you're ready to go!


ESSENTIAL CONTROLS
------------------

MOVING THE CURSOR:
  Left Stick = Move mouse cursor
  (Too fast or slow? Change the speed on the HIDra screen - it is remembered)

CLICKING:
  A Button = Left Click (select things)
  B Button = Right Click (open menus)
  Right Bumper (RB) = Double Click (open files/folders)
  
SCROLLING:
  Right Stick = Scroll up/down/left/right

UNDO:
  Left or Right Stick Click = Undo (Ctrl+Z)
  (Press down either thumbstick to undo last action)
  
SWAP MODES:
  Y Button = Swap sticks (cursor ↔ scroll)
  (Useful if you prefer right stick for cursor!)

VIRTUAL KEYBOARD:
  X Button = Open/close on-screen keyboard
  Left Stick = move the orange highlight between keys
  A Button   = Type the highlighted key
  B Button   = Type its shifted version (£ $ % : ? and CAPITALS)
  LB = Backspace   RB = Space   Y = Enter
  D-pad left/right = move the text cursor (to fix a mistake)
  Press the left stick in = numbers and symbols (again for letters)
  Start = Caps Lock   Back = Escape
  (from anywhere on the keyboard - the keys show LB, RB and Y on them.
   Close the keyboard and they all do their usual jobs again)
  (Big UK layout, letters offset like a real keyboard)
  123 #+ key (bottom left) = numbers and symbols. abc = back to letters
  Green row at the top = Word suggestions. Push up to reach them:
               A types the whole word, B types it with a Capital
  Right Stick  = Jump: up to the green words, right to the shortcuts,
                 left or down back to the letters
  Left Trigger = Tap: move the keyboard to the top/bottom of the screen.
                 Hold it and push the left stick: drag the keyboard anywhere
  (use it when the keyboard covers what you are typing)

SWITCHING BETWEEN PROGRAMS:
  1. Tap Left Bumper (LB) - Opens window switcher (Alt+Shift+Tab)
  2. Keep tapping LB to find your program
  3. Release when you see the program you want
  (It automatically switches after a brief delay)

WINDOW MANAGEMENT (D-PAD)
--------------------------

D-Pad Up = MAXIMIZE WINDOW
  Makes the current window full-screen

D-Pad Down = MINIMIZE WINDOW
  Sends the current window to the taskbar

D-Pad Left = SNAP LEFT
  Puts window on left half of screen

D-Pad Right = SNAP RIGHT
  Puts window on right half of screen

  TIP: Use snap left/right to work with two programs side-by-side!

COMMON TASKS
------------

Open Start Menu:
  Press Back Button

Open a Program:
  1. Press Back (opens Start Menu)
  2. Move Left Stick to the program
  3. Press A to open it

Type Text:
  1. Click into the box you want to type in
  2. Press X Button (opens the keyboard)
  3. Use the LEFT STICK to move the orange highlight to a letter
     (hold a direction to travel quickly across the keyboard)
     (D-pad up and down also move it; left and right move the text cursor)
  4. Press A to type the highlighted letter
  5. Press B for the symbol printed ABOVE the key,
     or for a CAPITAL letter (e.g. A gives 3, B gives £)
     Numbers and most symbols are behind the 123 #+ key
  6. Press X again to close the keyboard
     (Keyboard covering your work? Press Left Trigger to move it)

  You do NOT need to aim the mouse at the keys any more.
  While the keyboard is open the left stick moves the highlight
  instead of the mouse, and A types instead of clicking.
  If you need to click something, hold the Right Trigger.

Work with Two Programs:
  1. Open first program, press D-Pad Left (snap left)
  2. Open second program, press D-Pad Right (snap right)
  3. Now both programs are visible side-by-side!

See All Open Programs:
  Press Start Button (shows all windows)

FULL BUTTON REFERENCE
----------------------

These are the default jobs. Any button can be given a different job in
Settings, Buttons. The Guide tab of the HIDra screen always shows
what YOUR buttons do - press one and it lights up.

Left Stick:
  Move = Mouse cursor
  Click Down = Undo (Ctrl+Z)

Right Stick:
  Move = Scroll. While the keyboard is open: jump - up to the green
         words, right to the shortcuts, back to the letters
  Click Down = Undo (Ctrl+Z)

Triggers:
  Left Trigger (LT) = While the keyboard is open: tap to move it between
                      the top and bottom of the screen, or hold LT and
                      push the left stick to drag it anywhere. While it is closed, whatever it
                      is set to in Settings, Buttons - normally Escape (closes
                      a menu or box)
  Right Trigger (RT) = Click and hold (hold to drag things)

Face Buttons:
  A = Left Click (select/open)
  B = Right Click (context menu)
  X = Toggle Virtual Keyboard (UK layout, big letters)
  Y = Swap Stick Modes (cursor ↔ scroll)

Bumpers:
  Right Bumper (RB) = Double Click
  Left Bumper (LB) = Window switcher (hold to browse, A to choose)

D-Pad:
  Up = Maximize Window (full screen)
  Down = Minimize Window (to taskbar)
  Left = Snap Window Left (left half of screen)
  Right = Snap Window Right (right half of screen)

Back/Start:
  Back = Windows Key (Start Menu)
  Start = Task View (see all windows at once)

PRACTICE
--------
The Practice tab of the HIDra screen has two tests. Each is the same
every time, so you can see yourself getting better:
  Pointer test  Click 12 circles, one at a time. Some are further down
                or up the page - scroll with the right stick to find
                them. The circles get smaller as you get better.
  Typing test   Type each word, phrase or sentence. The box moves
                between the top and bottom - if the keyboard covers
                it, press LT to move the keyboard.
Every run is kept, for looking back on, and can be saved as a
spreadsheet with "Save for a review...".

VIRTUAL KEYBOARD TIPS
----------------------
- Letters, and the punctuation sentences need ( ' , . / ), are on
  the main keys. 123 #+ swaps them for numbers and symbols in the same
  places: 1-0, - = [ ] ; # \ €, and ! @ £ & ( ) : " on their own keys.
  Every key round the edge stays put, so abc is right where 123 was
- UK layout with " above 2 and £ above 3
- Shift symbols are big and easy to see
- Press B for symbols and capitals - no need to visit Shift at all
- Shift still works if you prefer it, and clears after one key
- Press Caps Lock (on the 123 #+ keys) for ALL CAPS typing
- Press Ctrl then another key for shortcuts
- The bottom row also has Ctrl, Home, End and the arrows. Win, PgUp
  and PgDn are on the 123 #+ keys
- Delete sits beside Backspace
- Home and End jump to the start or end of a line, which saves
  pressing the arrow keys once per letter
- Sentences, and I, start with a capital automatically. Press B
  (or Shift) first for a small letter instead
- The PHRASES key at the start of the green row swaps the
  suggestions for your saved phrases (name, email...). They are set
  in Settings, Phrases, on the HIDra screen
- Keyboard covering your text? Press Left Trigger to move it, or
  switch on "Fade when resting" so it goes see-through when you pause
- The keys on the right are SHORTCUTS - one press each - in rows:
    Edit    Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste
    Select  Select, Select all, word left/right, Delete word
    Style   Bold, Italic, Underline, Bigger, Smaller
    Tools   Emoji, Captions, Voice (speak instead of typing), Save, Snip
  EMOJI shows the keyboard's own emoji in place of the letters; abc
  brings the letters back.
  The keys in each row can be changed in Settings, Shortcut keys -
  e.g. Read, Print, Magnify, Clipboard, Find - or leave
  a place empty. The bottom row changes to suit the program in front,
  and says which:
  Slides (PowerPoint), Word, Web, Folders (File Explorer) or Page
- CAPTIONS turns on Windows Live Captions: words on screen for any
  sound on the PC - videos, calls. Press it again to turn them off
- READ (if it has been added) says the selected words out loud -
  select them first (Select, then move). Press READ again to stop.
  Whatever you had copied is kept
- SNIP takes a picture of part of the screen (drag with RT), or pick
  whole screen from the bar at the top. It is copied, ready to Paste
- SELECT (the highlighter key) turns orange: now the arrows and word
  keys highlight text as they move. Then press Copy, Cut, Bold or
  just type. B on an arrow selects one step
- APPS (top left) shows your programs with their logos - PowerPoint,
  Word, Edge... - one press opens it
- Word suggestions appear as you type, and guess the NEXT word
  after a space. They start fresh after arrows, Home, End or Tab,
  because the keyboard cannot see where the text cursor went

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

Controller stopped working?
  HIDra reconnects by itself. Plug the controller back in, or turn it
  back on, and wait a few seconds - it will pick it up automatically.
  You do not need to click anything.

Battery warning appeared?
  Change or charge the controller battery now, while it still works.
  A cable is the most reliable option - it cannot go flat.

Window disappeared?
  HIDra is still running. Hold BACK and START together for one second
  and the window comes back. You can also click the HIDra icon in
  the notification area, next to the clock.

Closing the window does NOT stop HIDra.
  It keeps running so the controller keeps working. To stop it properly,
  right-click the HIDra icon near the clock and choose "Exit HIDra".

A button does something unexpected?
  It may have been given a different job. The Guide tab shows what
  each button does now. "Reset all" in Settings, Buttons puts
  every button back. Holding BACK and START together always brings
  HIDra back, whatever they have been given.

Cursor moving too fast/slow?
  - Use the - and + buttons beside Pointer speed, in Settings, Pointer
    of the HIDra screen
  - Your speed is remembered for next time

Need help?
  - All buttons are shown in this guide
  - Everything works the same every time
  - No configuration files to worry about!
